# R-1.2 — Tender Totals: final recommendation (**FOR OWNER DECISION — NOT IMPLEMENTED, NOT TRIAL-VERIFIED**)

- **Status:** a recommendation with the exact amendment surface. Unlike R-1.1 (which was proven by a trial amendment, see `R-1-REPORTING-AMENDMENT-SPEC.md`
  §3), **R-1.2 has not been built or run**: its central question is a policy ruling that only the owner can make (manual card / other refunds).
  Every frozen Tender file is byte-identical to `HEAD`.
- **Baseline today (measured):** the frozen Tender Totals report shows **no** anonymous refund of any kind — refund count and total stay 0 and the net tender
  movement is unchanged by an attested cash refund. Its `reconciled: true` is correct for the four journal kinds it reads and silent about the rest. The
  anonymous credit-note **summary** (implemented, verified) already carries all four situations below, reconciled to the ledger.

## 1. The four refund situations — what each is, and the recommended treatment

| Situation                                     | What exists in the system                                                                                                                                     | Is it a tender movement?                   | Recommended treatment in the Tender report                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cash / bank refund EXECUTED** (attested)    | an `EXECUTED` refund row **and** a sealed execution journal `Dr ASSET.PAYMENT_CLEARING / Cr ASSET.CASH_ON_HAND \| ASSET.BANK` (database-guaranteed since F-4) | **Yes** — money left a tender account      | **Journal-anchored, like every other movement.** A new movement kind `anonymous_credit_note_refund`; period = its journal's `postingDate`; joined to its refund row by primary key; counted by the density guard; reconciled to the ledger (new shape rule, §3).                                   |
| **Refund PENDING** (issued, not yet attested) | a `PENDING` refund row; **no** execution journal (the money is booked in flight by the credit note's _issuance_ journal)                                      | **No** — nothing has left a tender account | **Never added to `refunds` or `netTenderMovement`.** Shown only in a new additive block `anonymousRefunds.pending` (count, amount, by method) labelled "not money out".                                                                                                                            |
| **Refund FAILED** (attested as not paid)      | a `FAILED` refund row; no journal; its amount stays unallocated on the credit note                                                                            | **No**                                     | **Never a movement.** Shown only in `anonymousRefunds.failed` and `anonymousRefunds.unallocated` (the credit still owed).                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Manual card / other refund ATTESTED**       | an `EXECUTED` refund row; **no journal** (approved two-phase design — the sale debited `PAYMENT_CLEARING`, so the issuance credit is already final)           | **Economically yes; in the ledger, no**    | **Cannot be journal-anchored.** Recommended for v1: **exclude from the movements and the ledger controls, but disclose**: `anonymousRefunds.manuallyAttested` (count, amount, by method) carrying an explicit, permanent label "attested by a user; **no journal, not reconciled to the ledger**". |

Also shown, additively: `anonymousRefunds.issued` (all refund lines of the notes issued in the period, by status) so that issued = pending + executed + failed
is visible, exactly as in the summary.

## 3. Exact amendment surface (to be built only after T-1…T-4 are decided)

**Statement (`tender-totals-report.sql.ts`).** Stage by stage:

1. `TENDER_SOURCE_KINDS` gains `anonymousRefund`, **imported** from its producer's constant (`ANONYMOUS_REFUND_EXECUTION_SOURCE_KIND`), exactly as the Sales
   amendment imports its kind.
2. `cand` (density candidates) and `sel`: the kind joins the `IN (…)` lists — an executed anonymous cash / bank refund is **one logical movement** for the
   100 000-movement guard.
3. New stage `arf` (like `rf`): `LEFT JOIN "anonymous_credit_note_refund" ar ON ar.id = s."docId"` (explicit tenant + company), `WHERE s."sourceKind" = <kind>`
   and the branch predicate; `expKey` is a **second, explicit mirror** of the frozen tender → account map: `CASH → ASSET.CASH_ON_HAND`, `BANK_TRANSFER → ASSET.BANK`
   (a parity pin ties it to `resolveReceiptAccountKeyForTender`, as the database guard's parity test already does).
4. `jl2` unions the new stream; `gl` groups it; a **new shape control `arfs`** (the counterpart of `rfs`): per journal, `Dr ASSET.PAYMENT_CLEARING = amount`,
   `Cr expKey = amount`, nothing else — **not** the `Dr LIABILITY.CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` shape that a `Refund` journal has. `ic` gains the orphan and shape counters;
   a mismatch fails the report closed, like every existing one.
5. `anonymousRefunds` (additive, T-1 b / T-3): a separate read of `anonymous_credit_note` and `anonymous_credit_note_refund` for the issued / pending / failed /
   manually-attested figures. This **adds two tables to a statement pinned to "exactly these tables"** — the single largest pin change.

**Pins and gates that change:** pin C (the kind list, the table list, the refund-account case, the control set), the byte-hash constants of
`tender-totals-report.{sql.ts,ts,repository.ts}` in pins **D, E, F**, decision-log rows **3b.10-TT / 3b.10-TD** (amended in place with a dated owner line), the
**Tender integration oracle** (3 445 lines), and the **performance gates**: the 100 000-movement dense-window and query-plan suites **must be re-measured** —
a previous version of this statement had a measured JIT-compilation defect at 100 000 movements, and any change to its cost profile must be re-proven.

## 4. What R-1.2 would and would not give the accountant

- **Gives:** executed anonymous cash / bank refunds in the tender report, reconciled to the ledger with a shape check; pending, failed and issued lines
  visible and never mistaken for money out; manual-card attestations disclosed.
- **Does not give (without T-2 Q or R):** manual card / other refunds **in** the movements or the ledger controls — by the approved two-phase design they post no
  journal, so a ledger-reconciled figure for them does not exist.
- **Already available today, without any frozen change:** the summary (`/anonymous-credit-note-summary`) reports **all four** situations, the manual-card
  attestations included (by attestation date), with its own ledger reconciliation and the adjustments to subtract from the frozen report.

## 5. Recommendation

**Do not start R-1.2 now.** For the pilot, keep R-1.0 (the summary). If the owner wants the frozen Tender report to reflect anonymous refunds, approve **T-1(b)**,
**T-2(P)** and **T-3** and schedule R-1.2 **after** R-1.1 is merged and proven; treat **T-2(R)** as part of the accountant's P1 / P2 decision, not as a reporting
change. Until then **no frozen Tender file, pin hash or decision-log row is changed.**
